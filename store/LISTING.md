## Full description (4000 max)

```
Spark Gym is a training log that shows you what you are actually doing — not
what you meant to do.

Every set you record lights up a real anatomical 3D body. Muscles you have
trained enough glow warm; the ones you keep skipping stay cold. Rotate it, tap
any muscle, and it tells you which exercises train it — with equipment and
without.

WHAT IS INSIDE

• 239 exercises, each with the muscles it drives, the ones it assists, and how
  to do it. Barbell, dumbbell, machine, cable, band — and a full no-equipment
  half of the library for training at home.
• 14 bundled programmes plus a builder for your own, with folders, supersets
  and a rest timer.
• A 3D muscle heat map built from a real anatomical model, shaded by your last
  7 or 30 days of work.
• Personal records tracked automatically, with volume history.
• A food diary with 192 foods, barcode lookup, macro targets worked out from
  your own TDEE, water tracking and 8 meal plans that scale to your numbers.
• A coach that reads your own logs — deload calls, plateau detection, weak-point
  audits. Rules over your data, not a chatbot.
• Levels, ranks, six attributes and daily targets, if that is what keeps you
  turning up.
• Progress photos, side by side over time.

IT WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL

There is no account and no server. Everything you log is stored on your phone,
including the 3D model — the heat map works in a basement gym with no bars.
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.

WEARABLES, OPTIONAL

Connect Health Connect and Spark Gym reads steps, heart rate, sleep and calories
from whatever writes them — Fitbit, Samsung Health, Wear OS, Garmin. Finished
workouts can be written back so the rest of your phone agrees. You can also link
a Fitbit account directly. Both are optional; the app is complete without them.

BAHASA INDONESIA

Antarmuka penuh dalam Bahasa Indonesia dan Inggris, bisa diganti kapan saja.

Free, no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases.
```
`~1,750 characters`
